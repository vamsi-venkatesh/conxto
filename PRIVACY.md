# Privacy, in plain words

- Your résumé is read once and kept as you uploaded it. Tailored versions are written per application and shown next to the original.
- Facts about you, such as work authorization or notice period, are stored only after you confirm them.
- Job postings are collected from public sources and company career pages. Conxto shows title, company, place and a link to the original.
- AI calls for free accounts go through free providers with your name and contact details replaced before the text leaves our server. Which providers, and their data-retention terms, are still being written up on the site's privacy page; until then, ask us.
- The Gmail permission is send only. Conxto cannot read your inbox. Disconnect from Profile at any time; the token is revoked at Google too.
- Nothing is sent to an employer without your click.
- Delete and export are available from your account, as required by the GDPR. The legal pages are on conxto.com.
